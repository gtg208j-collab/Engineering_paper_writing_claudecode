### Simulation and Experimental Evidence

The presentation reports a 25-UAV triangular-formation simulation, communication-rate comparisons, packet-loss and delay measurements, known-obstacle avoidance, and circular formation at 1 m altitude with a 2 m target radius.

### Quantitative Results

The reported communication-rate examples show 6 collisions and 58 warnings at 1 packets/s, and 242 collisions and 373 warnings at 0.5 packets/s. Reported cluster packet-loss rates are 4.45%, 4.20%, and 4.16%; circle rates are 2.84%, 2.61%, and 2.52%. Reported average delays are 167, 680, and 1130 microseconds for cluster and 146, 287, and 429 microseconds for circle. These values require raw-log and repeated-trial verification.
